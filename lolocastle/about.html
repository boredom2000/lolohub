What is Lolo Castle?

Lolo Castle is a 3D castle-building game where you defend your home from an army that never stops coming. Your castle starts in the middle of a big map. Enemies walk in from every edge, and every two minutes a giant boss joins them. How long can your castle stand?

Every game is played on a new random map, so no two battles are the same.

How to play

  • Make gold first. Build farms, wharfs and mines. When one is ready, click or tap its coin to collect the gold.
  • Protect the castle. Spend your gold on walls to slow enemies down and towers to shoot them.
  • Train troops. Troop buildings send soldiers out to fight for you.
  • Watch the health bar at the top. When it is empty, the game is lost and a new map starts.

On a computer

  • Left-drag or W A S D to move around the map
  • Right-drag or Q E to rotate, mouse wheel to zoom
  • 1 to 9 and 0 to pick a building, Esc to stop building

On a phone or tablet

  • Drag to move, pinch to zoom and rotate
  • Pick a building at the bottom, tap or drag its ghost where you want it, then press ✓
  • Tap the coins to collect gold

Tips

  • Spend your first gold on more farms and mines: a strong economy wins long games.
  • Enemies follow the easiest path to the castle. Use walls to lead them past your towers.
  • Save some gold before the two-minute mark so you are ready for the boss.
