What is Alien in Space?

Alien in Space is a fast arcade game on a rocky alien planet. Little aliens sneak out from behind the rocks and fall from the sky, and you are the only one who can stop them. Each wave brings more aliens than the last. How many waves can you survive?

How to play

  • Aim at the aliens and fire before they reach you.
  • Your health bar is at the top. When the aliens hit you, it goes down.
  • Zap aliens to raise your score. Your best score is saved on your device.

Your five space weapons

  • Plasma Rifle: quick shots, good all-round.
  • Laser Gun: one huge beam, then a 3-second recharge.
  • Rocket Launcher: explodes and hits groups of aliens.
  • Flamethrower: hold to spray close-up aliens.
  • Machine Gun: hold for a stream of fast shots.

On a computer

  • Mouse to aim, hold click to fire
  • 1 to 5 or the mouse wheel to switch weapons

On a phone or tablet

  • Touch to aim, hold to fire
  • Tap a weapon at the bottom of the screen to switch

Tips

  • Keep an eye on the rocks: aliens love to pop out from behind them.
  • Use the rocket when aliens bunch up, and the laser on the toughest ones.
  • Switch weapons while one is recharging instead of waiting.
