What is Lolo Hub?

Lolo Hub is a small collection of free games made for kids and families. Every game runs right in the web browser, on phones, tablets and computers, without installing anything.

The games are made by an independent developer. Each one is built from scratch: the worlds, the characters and the sound effects are made in code, so the games load fast and stay small.

Our games

  • Castle: build and defend a castle.
  • Alien in Space: stop the alien waves.
  • Hook: swing and fly with a grappling hook.
  • Kart: race karts on wild tracks.
  • Super Baking: run a busy bakery.
  • Foot: wacky 4 vs 4 football.

New games and new levels are added over time.

Safe for kids

  • No accounts, no chat and no way to talk to strangers.
  • No personal information is asked for or collected. See the privacy policy.
  • The games are free. Short ads from Google may play between rounds; they are never personalized.
