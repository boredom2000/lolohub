What is Crowd Rush?

Crowd Rush is a running game with a bit of math. Your crowd of runners keeps running forward. Steer it through the gates: blue gates (+ and ×) add runners, red gates (− and ÷) take them away. Then crash into the red team: whoever has more runners wins. Every third level ends with a giant!

How to play

  • Before each pair of gates, think: which one gives you the MOST runners? (With 8 runners, ×2 makes 16 but +10 makes 18!)
  • Stay away from the pink spinning saws.
  • At the finish, every runner left is worth 10 points.
  • You have 3 lives for the 3 levels of a round. The next round starts harder.

On a computer

  • Move the mouse left and right, or use ← →

On a phone or tablet

  • Slide your finger left and right anywhere on the screen

Tips

  • × is better when you have lots of runners, + is better when you have few.
  • Big red crowds need a big crowd: grab the good gates before them.
