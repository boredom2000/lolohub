What is Wiggle.io?

Wiggle.io is a snake game in a big round arena with 11 computer snakes. Eat the glowing dots to grow. If a snake bumps its head into another snake's body, it pops into lots of dots. After 3 minutes, the biggest snake wins!

How to play

  • Steer around the arena and eat dots. Big dots are worth more.
  • Hold boost to go fast. Boosting makes you a little shorter.
  • Cut in front of another snake so it bumps into you, then eat what it leaves behind.
  • Touching the pink wall or another snake pops you, and you come back small. Your biggest length still counts.

On a computer

  • Move the mouse to steer (or ← →)
  • Hold the mouse button or Space to boost

On a phone or tablet

  • Drag anywhere to steer
  • Hold the BOOST button to go fast

Tips

  • When you are big, circle around a small snake to trap it.
  • Stay away from the heads of big snakes.
  • Look at the mini map to find a quiet corner.
