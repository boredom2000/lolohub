What is Merge Moon?

Merge Moon is a dropping and merging puzzle. You drop space things into a jar. When two of the same touch, they squish together into the next bigger one: ✨ spark, ☄️ comet, 🪨 rock, 🌙 moon, 🧊 ice world, 🌍 earth, 🪐 ringed planet, ☀️ sun, 🌟 super star, 🌌 galaxy and the 🕳️ black hole. Two black holes make a giant blast!

How to play

  • Aim, then drop. The next thing to drop is shown in the corner.
  • Merges right after each other make a chain and multiply your points.
  • If the pile stays over the pink line too long, the jar is full and the game ends.
  • SHAKE bumps everything in the jar. You start with 3 and earn one more every 1,500 points.

On a computer

  • Move the mouse or ← → to aim
  • Click, Space or ↓ to drop, X to shake

On a phone or tablet

  • Drag to aim, lift your finger to drop
  • Tap 🫨 SHAKE to shake the jar

Tips

  • Keep the small things together on one side and the big ones on the other.
  • Look at what comes next before you drop.
  • Don't leave a tiny thing stuck under a big one.
