What is Gem Pop?

Gem Pop is a match-3 puzzle. Swap two gems that sit next to each other so that 3 or more of the same line up: they pop and new gems fall from the top. You have 25 moves. Score 2,000 for one star, 4,000 for two and 6,500 for three!

Special gems

  • 4 in a row makes a line blaster: when it pops, it clears a whole row or column.
  • An L or a T shape makes a bomb 💥 that blows up everything around it.
  • 5 in a row makes a spinning rainbow. Swap it with any gem to clear every gem of that kind. Two rainbows together clear the whole board!
  • Gems that fall into new matches make a combo: each step of the combo multiplies the points.

On a computer

  • Drag a gem onto its neighbour, or click one gem then the one next to it
  • Keyboard: ← → ↑ ↓ to pick a gem, X to grab it, then an arrow to swap

On a phone or tablet

  • Swipe a gem toward the neighbour you want to swap with, or tap one then the other

Tips

  • Make matches near the bottom: falling gems can start free combos.
  • Save rainbows for the gem you see the most.
  • Stuck for a few seconds? Two gems will wiggle to show you a move.
