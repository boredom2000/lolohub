What is Lolo Kart?

Lolo Kart is a 3D kart racing game with cartoon karts. Choose your driver, pick a track and race seven rivals over three laps. You start at the back of the grid, so you will have to overtake everyone to win!

Drivers

There are 8 drivers, and each kart drives differently:

  • Balanced: good at everything, great for your first races.
  • Zippy: quick to speed up and easy to turn.
  • Heavy: slow to start but hard to push around.
  • Wild: very fast and a little hard to control.

Tracks

  • Sunny Circuit: a wide, friendly first track with boost pads and cheering fans. The best place to learn.
  • Candy Canyon: bouncy candy hills, lollipop trees and a big jump over a chocolate river.
  • Cloud Coaster: a roller coaster in the sky with a giant climb, a corkscrew and a section with no rails!
  • Volcano Vault: a figure-8 over a lava lake, with a high bridge and two jumps.

Your best time on each track is saved on your device. More tracks are coming!

On a computer

  • W A S D or the arrow keys to drive and steer
  • Space to brake, R to get back on the track
  • Esc or P to pause

On a phone or tablet

  • Turn the wheel on the screen to steer
  • Hold GAS to go and BRAKE to slow down

Tips

  • Drive over the boost pads for a burst of speed, especially on jump ramps.
  • Let go of the gas a little before sharp turns instead of hitting the walls.
  • Fell off or stuck? Press R to get back on the track.
  • Try every driver: the best kart is different on each track.
