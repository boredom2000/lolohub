What is Lolo Swarm?

Lolo Swarm is a "survive the horde" game. Your little hero shoots by itself, so all you do is move: dodge the monsters, scoop up the glowing gems they drop and choose a new power each time you level up. Two bosses show up, at 2 and 4 minutes. Last 5 minutes to win!

How to play

  • Move away from the monsters and let your weapons do the work.
  • Grab the gems to fill the green bar at the top. When it is full you level up and pick one of 3 powers.
  • Powers: Zap Gun, Orbiters, Nova, Storm (weapons) and Zoom Shoes, Big Heart, Magnet, Power, Haste. Each goes up to level 5.
  • A ❤️ heals you. Beating a boss gives two free powers.

On a computer

  • WASD or the arrow keys to move
  • 1 2 3 to pick a power, Esc for the hub

On a phone or tablet

  • Put your finger anywhere and drag: it works like a joystick
  • Tap a card to pick a power

2 players: TEAM UP

Pick 2P TEAM UP and survive together! Each hero fires the weapons by itself, and you share the gems, the levels and the powers. If one hero falls, the other has to stay alive for 10 seconds and then the fallen hero comes back. An invisible rope keeps you both on the same screen. There are more monsters, so stick together!

  • P1: W A S D · P2: the arrow keys
  • Tablet: each player drags on their half of the screen

Tips

  • Keep moving in big circles: the monsters follow you in a line.
  • Take a new weapon early, then make your favourite one stronger.
  • The Magnet makes gems come to you, so you can stay safe.
