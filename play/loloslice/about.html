What is Slice Storm?

Fruit flies up from the bottom of the screen. Swipe across it to slice it in half! You have 3 lives: you lose one each time a fruit falls back down without being cut, and each time you slice a 💣 bomb.

How to play

  • Cut 3 or more fruit in one swipe for a combo bonus.
  • Golden fruit needs 5 quick cuts, and gives lots of points.
  • 🧊 Ice slows everything down for a few seconds.
  • 🌟 Star starts a fruit frenzy: fruit everywhere!
  • The longer you last, the more fruit (and bombs) come at once.

On a computer

  • Hold the mouse button down and swipe across the fruit

On a phone or tablet

  • Swipe your finger across the fruit

Tips

  • Wait for fruit to line up at the top of its jump, then slice them all in one go.
  • Lift your finger when a bomb is near, then swipe again around it.
