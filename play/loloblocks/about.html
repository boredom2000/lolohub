What is Block Boom?

Block Boom is a block puzzle on an 8 × 8 board. You get 3 blocks at a time. Drag them onto the board. When a whole row or column is full, it explodes and you score. When none of your blocks fits anywhere, the game is over.

How to play

  • Drag a block from the tray at the bottom onto the board. The lines it would finish light up.
  • Clearing two or more lines at once makes a double, triple or mega boom.
  • Clear at least one line with every hand of 3 to keep your streak: it multiplies your points.
  • Empty the whole board for a +300 bonus.

On a computer

  • Drag the blocks with the mouse

On a phone or tablet

  • Drag the blocks with your finger: the block floats above your finger so you can see where it goes

Tips

  • Keep a space free for the big 3 × 3 square.
  • Fill rows near the edges first, and keep the middle open.
  • Think about all 3 blocks before placing the first one.
