What is Ball Bandits?

Your base sits in the middle of a big wild world. All around it, Lolo Balls lie in nests, and every nest is guarded by a wild animal. Grab a ball, run home before the animal catches you, and the ball hatches into a cute critter that makes coins every second. Three rival bandits are doing the same thing... and they will try to steal your critters! After 5 minutes, the richest base wins.

The wild world

  • Meadow (close to home): green balls, guarded by bunnies, ducks and squirrels. Easy to escape.
  • Forest: blue balls, guarded by foxes, boars, owls and badgers.
  • Snow, desert, volcano and swamp (far out): purple balls, guarded by wolves, bears, crocodiles and rhinos.
  • Legendary lairs in two far corners: a golden ball guarded by a dragon or a lion. Very fast!

How to play

  • Sneak: guards take naps (you see Zz). Grab a ball while the guard sleeps and you get a head start. An awake guard watches the circle around it and chases anyone who comes close.
  • Run: carrying a ball makes you slower. If the guard catches you, it takes the ball back and you are dizzy for a moment. Get far enough away, or reach any base, and it gives up.
  • Hatch: carry the ball into your base. It hatches after a few seconds and starts making coins.
  • Steal: walk into a rival base and grab one of their balls or critters, then run home with it. Carrying makes you slower!
  • Catch thieves: if a bandit grabs one of yours, follow the pink THIEF arrow and touch them to get it back.
  • Lock: stand in your base and press LOCK. For 15 seconds nobody else can get in. Then it needs 40 seconds to charge again.
  • Your base has 8 spots. When it is full, a better critter replaces your weakest one, which is sold for half its price.

Ball colors

  • Green: common, 1-2 coins a second.
  • Blue: rare, 4-7 coins a second.
  • Purple: epic, 13-20 coins a second.
  • Gold: legendary! 45-65 coins a second. Everybody will want it.

On a computer

  • W A S D or the arrow keys to move
  • Space to grab or steal, L to lock your base

On a phone or tablet

  • Drag anywhere to move
  • GRAB grabs or steals, LOCK locks your base

2 players

With 2 players the screen is split in two: P1 on the left, P2 on the right.

  • 2P TEAM UP: you share one base and its coins. One of you can guard home while the other goes raiding, and either of you can catch a thief.
  • 2P VERSUS: P2 takes over a rival base. Steal from each other! After 5 minutes the richer base wins.
  • P1: W A S D to move, Space to grab, G to lock
  • P2: the arrow keys to move, Enter to grab, . to lock
  • Tablet: each player uses their half of the screen, with their own GRAB and LOCK buttons

Tips

  • Fill your base with meadow critters first: coins every second add up fast.
  • Wait near a nest until the guard falls asleep, then grab and run!
  • The map in the corner shows where the balls are. Pink squares are animals chasing someone.
  • Steal when a bandit is far from home, and lock your base before you leave it.
