What is Sky Hop?

Froggy bounces all by itself. You only steer left and right, landing on platforms to climb higher and higher. Don't fall off the bottom of the screen! Your score is your height plus 10 points for every ⭐.

The sky

  • Green platforms are safe. Blue ones slide from side to side.
  • Orange platforms with cracks crumble: you fall right through them.
  • White clouds hold you once, then vanish.
  • Yellow springs launch you very high. 🚀 Jetpacks fly you up for a few seconds.
  • 🫧 Bubbles protect you from monsters for 8 seconds.
  • Land on a monster from above to stomp it (+3 ⭐). Bump into it from the side and it's over!
  • Go off one side of the screen and you come back on the other.

On a computer

  • ← → or A D to steer

On a phone or tablet

  • Hold the left or the right side of the screen to steer

Tips

  • Look up! Plan where you will land before you start falling.
  • Every 1,000 m the sky changes colour.
