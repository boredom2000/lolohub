What is Super Baking?

Super Baking puts you in charge of a busy little bakery on top of a giant slice of cake. Customers line up at the counter, and every step of their recipe is a quick mini-game: pour the flour, crack the eggs, whisk, knead, roll, bake and decorate. Serve as many orders as you can before the clock runs out!

How to play

  • Tap a customer to take their order. You can work on up to 3 orders at once, each with its own color.
  • The next step floats over a kitchen station. Tap the station (or its ticket at the bottom) to play that step's mini-game.
  • When every step is done, tap the customer to serve them.
  • Good mini-games add time to the clock: PERFECT +6s, GOOD +4s, OK +2s. Serving adds 5 seconds plus a tip for speed.
  • Don't keep customers waiting too long, or they will leave and take time off your clock!

The kitchen stations

  • Pantry: pour just the right amount.
  • Fridge: crack eggs and catch the good ingredients.
  • Mixer: shake, whisk and knead.
  • Prep: roll, shape and slice the dough.
  • Stove: melt chocolate and flip pancakes.
  • Oven: pull it out when it is golden.
  • Decor: frost, pipe, drop the cherry and add toppings.

Controls

  • Everything works with a tap or a click. Each mini-game shows what to do before it starts.
  • On a keyboard, 1 2 3 pick an order and play its next step.

Difficulty

Choose EASY, MEDIUM or HARD on the title screen. Easy gives you more time and bigger targets, which is perfect for younger bakers. Your best score is saved for each difficulty.
