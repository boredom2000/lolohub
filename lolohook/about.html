What is Lolo Hook?

Lolo Hook is a one-button swinging game. Throw your grappling hook at a glowing dot, swing, let go at the right moment and fly through the air! Get past the lasers and reach the EXIT portal to finish the level. There are 10 levels, from an easy first swing to tricky laser mazes.

How to play

  • Hold to grab the highlighted glowing dot with your hook.
  • Let go to fly. Let go at the bottom of a swing to go far, or near the top to go high.
  • Touching a laser or the floor sends you back to the start of the level. Just try again!
  • Your progress is saved. Use the LEVELS button to replay any level.

On a computer

  • Hold click or Space to hook, release to fly
  • R restarts the level, Esc goes back to the hub

On a phone or tablet

  • Touch and hold anywhere to hook, lift your finger to fly

Tips

  • Build up speed with a few swings before you let go.
  • The next dot lights up when it is in reach. Wait for it before you grab.
  • Count how many times you fall, then try to beat it on your next run!
