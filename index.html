How Lolo Hub works

  • Work hard. Short lessons that follow what kids learn at school this week, plus math practice. Each finished lesson earns gold.
  • Play hard. Every game costs a little gold per round. Longer rounds cost a bit more.
  • Mix it up. A lesson pays less each time you repeat it in the same week, so doing all of them pays best.
  • Guest or profile. Guests keep their gold until they close the page. On the home server you can make a profile (name, password, avatar) to keep it.
