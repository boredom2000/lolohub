What is Lolo Foot?

Lolo Foot is a wild 4 vs 4 football game: one goalkeeper and three players on each team. Your players run around on their own, and you only need one finger to play: tap to pass, hold to blast a super shot, tap to tackle. A match lasts two minutes. If it is a draw, the next goal wins!

How to play

When your team has the ball

  • The player with the ball has a yellow circle under him: that is the one you control.
  • Tap somewhere to kick the ball there. Tap near a teammate and the pass goes to him.
  • Hold to charge a kick. Let go on an empty spot for a super shot: a full charge sets the ball on fire, flies super fast and knocks over anyone in the way!
  • Hold on a teammate to lob the ball over the other team's heads.

When you don't have the ball

  • The player closest to the ball gets a blue circle.
  • Tap to make him dash that way. Dash into the player with the ball to tackle him and steal it.
  • After a dash you must wait about 2 seconds: the white ring shows when you can dash again.

Power-ups

  • 🍄 Giant: grow huge, stomp through players and nobody can tackle you.
  • ⚡ Turbo: run twice as fast.
  • 🔥 Fire ball: your team's next shot is a full-power fire shot.
  • ❄️ Freeze: the other team is frozen in ice for a few seconds.
  • 🥅 Mega goal: the other team's goal gets much bigger.
  • 🍌 Banana rain: banana peels around the other team. Slip!

And sometimes crazy things happen on the pitch: a cow runs across, a wind storm blows the ball, the gravity turns into moon gravity or the ball becomes HUGE.

Controls

  • On a phone or tablet: tap and hold with one finger. Turn your phone sideways for a bigger view.
  • On a computer: click and hold the mouse button.

Tips

  • Your player with the ball runs slowly. Pass before the other team catches you!
  • Charge your super shot when nobody is close, or the other team will tackle you first.
  • A red player with a glowing aura is about to shoot: dash at him quickly!
  • The goalkeeper saves easy shots. Aim for the corners and use fire shots.
