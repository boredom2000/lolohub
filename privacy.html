LOLO HUB

PRIVACY

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Lolo Hub is a website with free games for children and families. This page explains, in plain words, what information is used when you play. If you are a kid, please read it with a parent.

The short version

  • On the public site there are no accounts and no sign-up: everyone plays as a guest. There is no chat.
  • We do not ask for or collect email addresses, photos, locations or any other personal information.
  • Guest gold and game progress are saved only on your own device.
  • Ads are shown by Google and are never personalized.

What is saved on your device

Some games remember things like your best score, the levels you have finished, your chosen difficulty and whether sound is on. This is kept in your browser's local storage on your device and is never sent to us. If you press PLAY OFFLINE, a copy of the games is also saved in your browser so they work without internet.

You can delete all of it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

A guest's gold is kept in the browser tab only and is gone when the tab is closed.

Family profiles (home server only)

The Lolo family also runs a copy of Lolo Hub on its own home computer. Only there, a player can make a profile with a nickname, a password and a cartoon avatar, so their gold and finished lessons are kept. The password is stored scrambled (hashed), never in plain text. A profile holds nothing else, is never shared and never leaves that computer. Use a nickname, not a real full name.

Ads

To keep the games free, Lolo Hub uses Google AdSense to show ads, for example a short ad between two rounds of a game. Because Lolo Hub is made for children:

  • the site is marked as directed at children, and
  • ads are requested as non-personalized: they are not based on what you did on other websites.

Google and its partners may still use cookies or similar technology for things such as limiting how often an ad is shown, stopping fraud and counting ad views. You can read how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

In some regions (for example the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland) you will be asked for your choice about cookies before ads are shown.

Other services

The pages load their fonts from Google Fonts, and the site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any website, these services receive technical information such as your IP address and browser type so they can deliver the page. Lolo Hub does not use analytics or tracking tools of its own.

Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent and believe your child sent us personal information, for example by email, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
